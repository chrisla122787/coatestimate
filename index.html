How it works

1

Price it your way

Quick mode: add rooms at your usual flat price, or crew-days at your day rate, plus your materials, prep, travel, and other costs. Measured mode: enter room dimensions and CoatEstimate works out square footage, pooled paint gallons, primer, and labor hours — including prep.

2

See your profit first

Your total price, your estimated cost, your profit, and your true margin — privately, before the client sees anything. If a price falls below your target margin, CoatEstimate warns you and shows the minimum price that hits your target.

3

Send a branded estimate

Download a clean PDF estimate with your logo, the scope line by line, terms, and an acceptance block — ready to email to the homeowner. Your costs, rates, and margins never appear on it.

What you get

FREE

Quick estimates — the way you already price

Most repaints get priced from experience: a bedroom is $650, a kitchen is $450, the crew is $500 a day. Quick mode takes those numbers, adds your real costs (crew-days at your loaded labor cost, materials, prep, travel, other), and shows your profit and margin — with a profit-risk warning and the minimum price whenever a job prices below your target.

FREE

Measured estimates when you want them

Full measuring estimator: wall square footage from real dimensions, paint pooled across the whole job by surface and rounded up once per pool (the way you actually buy paint), primer as its own line, prep-level factors or your own prep hours, and every job cost — protection, travel, equipment, overhead. Override the hours or the final price anytime; the warning still guards your margin.

FREE

Watermarked estimate preview

See exactly how your estimate will look — your company name, the client, the scope by room, and the price — right on screen, free.

FREE

Your first PDF is free

Your first clean, branded PDF estimate is free — no watermark, your logo, ready to send. After that, PDFs are a Pro feature.

PRO

Professional PDF estimates

Your logo and contact info, scope and prices by area (or by quick line — room names and flat prices, no measurements), what's included and excluded, a deposit line, start-window wording, validity period, and a client acceptance block. Your paint prices, crew rate, costs, and margin never appear on the customer copy — scope and prices only.

PRO

Estimate dashboard & backups

Save every estimate, mark it Draft, Sent, Accepted, Declined, or Completed, and watch your pipeline and accepted-work totals add up. Export a backup file or a CSV anytime — your data stays in your browser, under your control.

The fine print, in plain words

Privacy

Your estimates, your company info, and your logo are stored only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing you type into the estimator is uploaded to us or anyone else — there is no account and no tracking of your numbers. Export a backup file from the app regularly, because clearing your browser data erases what's saved here.

Billing & cancellation

Subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from your Stripe receipt or the Stripe customer portal — no email to us required, no cancellation fee. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and the free estimator keeps working either way.
