Estimate mode

Quick is how most painters already price: enter your usual per-room price or your crew-days, add your costs, and see your profit before you send it. Switch to Measured when you want paint gallons, hours, and prep worked out from real measurements. Both modes produce the same branded estimate.

Quick lines & your costs

Add each room at the price you usually charge for it, or add crew-days at your day rate. Then enter what the job will actually cost you — CoatEstimate checks the margin on your usual prices.

Rooms & areas

Measure each space. Wall area = perimeter × ceiling height, minus 21 sq ft per door and 15 sq ft per window. Paint is pooled across the whole job by surface and rounded up once — you buy paint for the job, not room by room.
